About EE Rankings

EE Rankings is a metrics-based ranking of electrical engineering research, modeled on CSrankings. Rankings come from a count of the papers faculty publish in the most selective venues of each EE area, not from reputation surveys.

How is the score computed?

  1. Every paper in a counted venue gives each of its authors 1/(number of authors) credit, so a two-author paper gives each author 0.5.
  2. An institution's count in an area is the sum of that credit over its faculty, restricted to the chosen years.
  3. The score is the geometric mean of (count + 1) across the selected areas. This rewards breadth, and no single high-volume area can dominate.

Why are some areas unselected by default?

Machine learning, computer vision, robotics, and security draw many EE faculty, but computer science dominates their venues. If they counted by default, the ranking would mostly reflect CS strength, so they are marked adjacent and are off on first load. Select them, or click All, for a combined EE + CS view.

Where does the data come from?

Publications come from the DBLP bibliography. Affiliations come from DBLP person records, which are curated by the DBLP team and in many cases come from ORCID. Every affiliation string is mapped to a canonical institution and classified by department: EE/ECE (electrical, electronic, ECE, EECS, and similar), other (for example computer science or mechanical engineering), or unspecified.

Who counts as faculty?

DBLP does not record academic rank, so people are selected heuristically. A person must have a current academic affiliation, at least 7 years of publishing, at least 20 DBLP publications, recent activity, and at least 3 papers in counted venues. Curated corrections in data/faculty-overrides.csv always take precedence. Some senior researchers and postdocs may be included, and some faculty may be missing. Please submit corrections.

Why are devices, photonics, and power electronics thin?

Coverage is limited to venues indexed by DBLP. Several flagship EE venues are not indexed, including IEDM, CLEO, APEC, IMS, and the Antennas & Propagation symposium. These areas are therefore represented through the indexed venues closest to them: VLSI Technology and OFC for devices and photonics, and IEEE Transactions on Industrial Electronics for power electronics. Adding a venue is a one-line change in data/areas.json once DBLP indexes it.

How do I fix my entry?

Update your affiliation on DBLP (or ORCID), or add a line to data/faculty-overrides.csv / data/institution-aliases.csv and rebuild. See the README for the full pipeline.
